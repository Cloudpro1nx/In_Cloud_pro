## [English]

Welcome! This is a simple, lightweight, and secure cloud storage platform designed to safely store and manage your essential photos, audio files, and small videos up to 20MB. 🚀

### ✨ Key Features:
* 📁 Easily upload pictures, audio files, or small videos up to 20MB seamlessly.
* 🔒 Keep your essential files safe, organized, and accessible anytime you need them.
* ⚡ Fast, lightweight, and user-friendly interface.

### 📤 How to Upload Files:
1. Open the application and go to the home screen.
2. Look at the center of the screen and tap on the round plus (+) or upload button.
3. Select your desired photo, audio, or video file from your device storage.
4. Once selected, your file will be uploaded and safely stored in the cloud instantly!
